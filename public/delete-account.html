3. What Personal Data is Permanently Deleted

Upon account deletion (immediately in-app or within 7 days via request), Sri Sai permanently purges the following data from our live databases:

  • User Profile: Full name, phone number, email address, profile picture, and Google authentication identity.
  • Saved Addresses: All saved delivery locations, street names, flat/house numbers, building landmarks, and PIN codes in Anantapur.
  • Precise GPS Coordinates: Device latitude, longitude, and accuracy coordinates saved during checkout or location sharing.
  • Device Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens and push notification preferences.
  • Local Device Storage: In-app shopping cart items, recent searches, and cached sessions.

4. What Data is Retained and Why (Statutory Compliance)

Under Indian law, certain transaction records cannot be immediately erased:

  • Tax & Invoicing Records: Completed order invoices, transaction timestamps, items purchased, and payment gateway references (Razorpay payment IDs) are retained strictly as required by the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, Income Tax Act, 1961, and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 for statutory audit and accounting compliance.
  • Fraud Prevention & Audit: Basic order fulfillment logs are retained in encrypted, access-restricted format for the statutory retention period to resolve past disputes or chargebacks.
  • No Marketing Use: Retained transaction records are completely quarantined from any active promotional marketing, SMS, WhatsApp broadcasts, or push notifications.
