  • 515001 (Anantapur Main / Head Post Office / Tower Clock)
  • 515002 (Anantapur Engineering College / Subhash Road)

2. Delivery Timeframes & Scheduling

  • Same-Day Express Delivery: Orders placed during standard operating hours (7:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST) are packed fresh and dispatched within 30 to 60 minutes.
  • Scheduled Morning Slots: Orders placed when the store is closed for the night are scheduled for morning priority delivery starting at 7:00 AM IST.

3. Order Tracking

Customers can monitor the live progress of their order on the "My Orders" tab on our web app through each stage: Placed → Accepted → Packed → Out for Delivery → Delivered.

4. Delivery Issues or Delays

If our delivery executive cannot locate your address or reach your contact number, our store helpline (+91 62817 30144) will call or WhatsApp you directly to ensure successful handover.
