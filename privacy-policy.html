Privacy Policy — RA Store

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for Bommalatapalli, Andhra Pradesh

1. Data We Collect

When you place an order with RA Store, we collect only the necessary information to fulfill your grocery delivery:

  • Full Name: To address your delivery packages.
  • 10-Digit Mobile Number: For delivery rider contact and delivery coordination.
  • Delivery Address: To deliver items to your home in Bommalatapalli.
  • Google Account Email: To securely authenticate your account and track order history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for order processing, grocery packaging, delivery dispatch, and processing refunds through Razorpay. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Security & Storage

Authentication is handled directly through Google OAuth. Financial card and UPI information is processed securely by Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant); RA Store never stores your bank account or card numbers.

4. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

You have the right to request access to your personal data or ask for deletion of your profile by contacting the store owner directly via WhatsApp (+91 62817 30144) or visiting our storefront in Bommalatapalli.
