Privacy Policy — RA Store

Effective Date: October 2026 | Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh DPDP Act Compliant

1. Data We Collect

When you place an order with RA Store, we collect only the necessary information to fulfill your grocery delivery in Anantapur:

  • Full Name & Contact: To address delivery packages and coordinate doorstep handover.
  • 10-Digit Mobile Number: For WhatsApp order updates, OTP authentication, and delivery rider calls.
  • Delivery Address & Landmarks: Mandatory street, building, and landmark details in Anantapur (PINs 515001–515005).
  • Optional Precise GPS Location: If you explicitly tap "Share my location" at checkout, we capture your device latitude, longitude, and accuracy to help our delivery rider navigate directly to your house. This is 100% optional and only used for your delivery.
  • Doorstep Delivery Code: A cryptographic 4-digit code generated for proof of delivery to protect against misdelivery and fraud.
  • Account Details: Google Account email or WhatsApp verified phone for secure login and past order history.

2. Role-Based Access: Who Sees What

We enforce strict least-privilege security boundaries:

  • Delivery Riders / Staff: Can only view customer addresses, phone numbers, and location pins for active orders currently assigned to them. Once an order is marked delivered, delivery staff can no longer access your personal address or GPS coordinates.
  • Customer: You can only read your own orders and private delivery codes. Your delivery code is visible only while your order is Out for Delivery.
  • Store Owner: Retains encrypted audit logs for reconciliation, dispute resolution, and statutory compliance.

3. Location Data Retention & Deletion

Precise GPS coordinates are purged automatically from our databases 30 days after your order is delivered. You may also remove your saved coordinates or saved addresses at any time in your customer account profile.

4. Financial Data & Security

Payment card, UPI, and net banking transactions are processed securely by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). RA Store never stores or handles your bank account, card credentials, or UPI PINs.

5. Grievance Officer & Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal profile. For any privacy requests or inquiries, contact our Grievance Officer:
