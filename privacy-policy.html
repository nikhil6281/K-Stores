Privacy Policy — RA Store

Effective Date: October 2026 | Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh

1. Data We Collect

When you place an order with RA Store, we collect only the necessary information to fulfill your grocery delivery:

  • Full Name: To identify and address your delivery packages.
  • 10-Digit Mobile Number: For delivery rider contact and delivery coordination.
  • Delivery Address: To deliver groceries to your home or landmark in Anantapur.
  • Google Account Email: To securely authenticate your account and track order history.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for order processing, grocery packaging, delivery dispatch, and processing refunds through Razorpay. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Security & Financial Data

Authentication is handled directly through Google OAuth. Financial card, UPI, and banking information is processed securely by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant); RA Store never stores your bank account, UPI PIN, or card numbers.

4. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to request access to your personal data or ask for deletion of your customer profile by contacting our store manager via WhatsApp (+91 62817 30144) or emailing support@rastore.in.
