Cancellation & Refund Policy

RA Store — Bommalatapalli, Andhra Pradesh

1. Cancellations Before Dispatch

Customers can cancel an active order at any time while the status is Placed, Accepted, or Packed directly from the "My Orders" tab on our website.

2. 100% Automated Refunds

If you paid online via Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Cards), your cancellation triggers an automatic 100% refund of the entire order amount (including delivery fees) via the Razorpay Refund API. Funds return to your original payment method in 5 to 7 working days according to standard banking timelines.

3. Non-Cancellable State

Once an order has transitioned to Out for Delivery or Delivered, cancellations cannot be accepted as fresh groceries have already been dispatched with our delivery rider.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

For Cash on Delivery orders, cancellation before dispatch simply voids the order with zero cancellation fees.
