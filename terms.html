Terms of Service

Sri Sai — Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh

1. Service Area & Operational Hours

Sri Sai provides grocery and household delivery services within Anantapur (PIN: 515001 - 515005). Standard operational hours are 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST. Scheduled morning deliveries are accepted 24/7 for dispatch starting at 7:00 AM IST.

2. Pricing, Orders & Payment

All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR) at or below MRP. Order totals are re-calculated and verified server-side upon checkout. We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) and Online Payments (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) via Razorpay. For online transactions, orders are held in payment_pending until confirmed by the payment gateway.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time before dispatch (before moving to Out for Delivery). For paid online orders, an automatic refund is initiated upon cancellation and credited to the original payment method in 5–7 working days as per standard banking timelines.
