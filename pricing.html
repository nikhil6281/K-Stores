Pricing & Payment Policy

RA Store — Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh

1. Transparent Pricing (INR)

All prices listed on RA Store are in Indian National Rupees (₹ / INR) and inclusive of all applicable taxes. Items are priced strictly at or below Maximum Retail Price (MRP). We do not charge hidden packaging or handling fees.

2. Server-Side Price Verification

To ensure fairness and prevent errors, all item prices, applicable discounts, and delivery fees are re-calculated and verified on our server at the time of checkout. If a price was recently updated, you will be notified on-screen before confirming your order.

3. Accepted Payment Methods

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay in cash to our delivery executive when your groceries are handed over at your doorstep.
  • Online Payments (Razorpay): Pay securely using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), NetBanking, and authorized wallets via PCI-DSS compliant Razorpay gateway.

4. Delivery Fees & Free Delivery Threshold

Delivery fees are configured in store settings (typically ₹15 - ₹25 per order). Orders meeting our Free Delivery Threshold (typically ₹299 or above) receive 100% free doorstep delivery across Anantapur.
